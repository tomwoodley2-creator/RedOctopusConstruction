Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This policy explains what personal information Red Octopus Construction Ltd collects when you contact us through this website, how we use it and what your rights are. It is a starting template and should be reviewed by the business before publication.

Who we are

Red Octopus Construction Ltd (“we”, “us”) is the data controller for the information you provide through this site. You can reach us at paul@redoctopusconstruction.com or on 07861 147495.

What we collect

When you complete the quote or survey form we collect the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number, project address or postcode, the type of work you need and any message you send. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies on this site.

How we use it

We use these details only to respond to your enquiry — to contact you, arrange a site visit and prepare a quote. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form, and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries about our services.

Who sees it

Form submissions are delivered to us by Web3Forms, which processes the data on our behalf and passes it to us by email. We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else except where we are required to by law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to deal with your project and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To do so, contact us using the details above. If you are not happy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
